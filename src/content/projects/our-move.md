## Preparing before the emergency

Our Move focused on the work that happens before a warning is issued. Its
underlying idea was that preparation is easier when people can see how their
effort fits with the effort of neighbours and the wider community.

The project is now archived. This page intentionally does not direct visitors
to the former service domain.
