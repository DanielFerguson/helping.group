## Built for a specific moment

Helping Homes began during the 2019–20 Australian bushfire season. The first
question was practical: could people who had a spare room or property make it
easier for someone evacuating to find a safe place to stay?

That idea became a service built around an Australia-wide map of offers. The
current platform supports accommodation, paddock space and transport.

## A seasonal operating model

Helping Homes is not intended to manufacture urgency between disasters. It can
remain on standby, with the platform and public information kept ready for the
next event that requires it.

When the service is activated, Helping Group will publish the affected region,
the time of the latest update and links to official emergency information.
Helping Homes complements official services; it does not replace them.

## The trust boundary

Anyone in immediate danger should call 000 and follow advice from the relevant
emergency authority. Helping Homes is for connecting practical offers after
those immediate safety decisions have been made.
