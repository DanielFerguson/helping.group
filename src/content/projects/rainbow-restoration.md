## What the project explored

Rainbow Restoration asked whether a familiar, tangible format could create a
more approachable way to encounter LGBTQIA+ history.

The project remained a concept rather than becoming a public service. Its
archive page records the question it tried to answer without presenting it as
work that is still in development.
